On 7–8 May 2026, Rocío Da Riva presented Project RelNet at the **Semantic
Annotation for the Ancient World Conference 2026**, hosted by the TALOS Centre of
Excellence in AI for the Social Sciences and Humanities at the University of Crete,
in Rethymno, Greece.

The talk — *"Annotating temples, rituals and networks in Late Babylonia"* —
presented the project's approach to religious networks, sacred journeys, and
ritual practices in Babylon during the late first millennium BCE, and the methods
used to annotate and connect temples, rituals, and participants.
