On 10 March 2026, the RelNet team contributed a seminar on the cultic kinetics of
the *Divine Love Lyrics* at the Seminar für Altorientalistik, Friedrich Schiller
University, Jena.

The seminar examined the ceremonial movements and ritual choreography underlying
the *Divine Love Lyrics* — the recitations, actions, and processional itineraries
that the RelNet project maps across the sacred topography of Babylon.
