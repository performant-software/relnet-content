A University of Barcelona project analyses sacred journeys and the connections
between temples to better understand the society of the first millennium BCE.

The RelNet project (PID2022-136740NB-I00), directed by Serra Húnter professor
**Rocío Da Riva** at the University of Barcelona, studies religious networks in
late first-millennium Babylonia. It investigates how movements, itineraries, and
contacts between different centres of worship formed complex webs of relationships
in which religion structured social, political, and cultural life.
