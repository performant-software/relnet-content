      The study of pilgrimages in RelNet adopts a comprehensive approach encompassing any journey undertaken for religious purposes, regardless of distance or duration. This broad perspective is essential when examining ancient societies, where religious elements profoundly influenced all aspects of cultural and social life.

      The research focuses on two primary dimensions:

      **Typology and causes.** The project examines various pilgrimage types and their underlying motivations. While many journeys stemmed from participation in religious belief systems, others may have used religion merely as a framework or justification. We expect to discover diverse pilgrimage categories reflecting different intentions.

      **Ritualized elements.** Research identifies ceremonial aspects along pilgrimage routes, including sacrificial practices, worship-related objects, musical performances, specific clothing, particular foods, and travel provisions. These rituals occurred at various locations — way stations and sacred pathways leading to sanctuaries — requiring analysis within geospatial contexts.
