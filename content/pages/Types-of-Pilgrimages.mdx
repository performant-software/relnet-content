      The research focuses on two primary dimensions:

      **Typology and causes.** The project examines various pilgrimage types and their underlying motivations. While many journeys stemmed from participation in religious belief systems, others may have used religion merely as a framework or justification.

      **Ritualized elements.** Research identifies ceremonial aspects along pilgrimage routes — sacrificial practices, worship-related objects, musical performances, specific clothing, particular foods, and travel provisions — occurring at way stations and sacred pathways leading to sanctuaries, and analyzed within geospatial contexts.
