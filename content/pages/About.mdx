      RelNet studies religious networks in late first millennium BCE Babylonia, examining sacred travelers and ceremonial itineraries documented in cuneiform texts from temple and private archives.

      The project analyzes approximately 150 cuneiform texts to reconstruct the networks connecting cult centers, the movements of sacred travelers, and the spatial dimensions of religious festivals and ceremonies.

      RelNet is based at the Department of History and Archaeology, University of Barcelona, and is funded by the Spanish Ministry of Science and Innovation (PID2022-136740NB-I00).
