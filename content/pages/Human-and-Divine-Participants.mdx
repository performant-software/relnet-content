  - title: Performers, spectators, and deities
    description: >-
      The research identifies the people who participated in processions and
      pilgrimages, along with the divine entities involved in ceremonies, to better
      understand official religion's role in Babylonian society. It examines the
      roles and levels of participation of performers and spectators; the impact of
      official religion as a mechanism for social cohesion and cultural identity;
      and the deities involved in ceremonies, their originating religious centers,
      and their ceremonial functions — mapping relationships across the Babylonian
      pantheon. A gender-perspective analysis reassesses these ceremonies, their
      performative dimensions and ideological significance.
