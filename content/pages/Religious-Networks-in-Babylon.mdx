      The project pursues several key objectives:

      - Identify patterns in the articulation of sacred movements, focusing on cultural topography and the relationships between nodes in religious networks.
      - Provide data on the topography of intra- and inter-urban worship in Babylon, and on movements and contacts between places.
      - Describe the relationships between the axes — their direction, regularity, and possible modifications over time — and identify their causes.
      - Clarify the character of Esagil as a node in political-religious contacts.
      - Analyse the dynamics of religious processions and pilgrimages to elucidate the social function of festivals and places of worship.
      - Clarify the scope of royal euergetism and its impact on religious networks.
