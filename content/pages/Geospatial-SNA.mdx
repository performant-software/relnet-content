      Social network analysis (SNA) is a fundamental tool for data processing in RelNet. Because the relationships between actors in RelNet operate in space in all cases, geospatial integration is essential for comprehensive analysis.

      The methodology combines SNA with GIS capabilities. SNA allows the visual and schematic representation of documentary reality in graphs or matrices. By incorporating geospatial information, the project creates an integrated approach — geospatial SNA — to fully examine relational data involving individuals and places.
