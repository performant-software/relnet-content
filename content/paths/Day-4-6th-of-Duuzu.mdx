The fourth day of the itinerary, the 6th of Duʾūzu, brings the festival to a
close: from the House of Lamentation to the Equlû and Ekagula, through the lower
courtyard of the Eturkalamma, and finally to the Gate of the temple of Sîn for
wailing and ritual meals. Each stop pairs a place with the recitations and actions
recorded in the cuneiform sources. Reconstruction after Pedersén, O. (2021),
*Babylon Maps*, Babylon: The Great City (Münster: Zaphon).
