Day 4 opens with riksu riksu and recitations in the House of Lamentation: “Where will they see you?” and “To the Equlû.” The procession then moves to the Equlû, where performers stand before Ninayātu and recite “Ḫursagkalamma.” The action proceeds to the Ekagula in the Esagil complex and then to the Eturkalamma; after a lacuna, “Kār-Bēl-mātāti” is chanted, followed by assinnu dancing and flute playing. Still in the Eturkalamma’s lower courtyard, the ritual moves to the gate of Sîn’s temple for lamentations and ritual meals before returning to the Eturkalamma.
