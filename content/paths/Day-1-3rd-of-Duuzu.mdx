On Day 1\* (Duʾūzu 3) the ritual action begins inside the Esagil with lamentations and recitations concerning Zarpānītu, Bēl, and Ištar. It then moves to the Eturkalamma, Ištar of Babylon’s temple, where recitations are performed at the gates of Bēltiya and Madānu. The ritual actors proceed before Bēltiya (Zarpānītu) and then into Bēl’s cella in the Esagil. Bēl is then said to go from Bēltiya to Esalsukkal, where the recitation “He frightens me” is performed. The ceremony ends at noon on the third of Duʾūzu; it occurs entirely within the temples’ inner spaces, accessible only to priests and ritual personnel. Reconstruction after R. Da Riva and J. Socorregut, 2026.
