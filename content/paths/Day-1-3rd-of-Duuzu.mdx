The first day of the itinerary, the 3rd of Duʾūzu, moves from the Ekagula through
the lower courtyard of the Eturkalamma to the Esalsukkal, as Bēl goes out from
before Bēltiya. Each stop pairs a place with the recitations and actions recorded
in the cuneiform sources. Reconstruction after Pedersén, O. (2021), *Babylon
Maps*, Babylon: The Great City (Münster: Zaphon).
