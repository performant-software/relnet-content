The second day of the itinerary, the 4th of Duʾūzu, is the longest circuit: from
the Eturkalamma out to the Equlû and Ekagula, back along the street of the
Eturkalamma to the garden and the Gate of Bēltiya, and finally to the Gate of the
Rābiṣu by the river. Each stop pairs a place with the recitations, actions, and
participants recorded in the cuneiform sources. Reconstruction after Pedersén, O.
(2021), *Babylon Maps*, Babylon: The Great City (Münster: Zaphon).
