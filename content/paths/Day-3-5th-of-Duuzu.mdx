The third day of the itinerary, the 5th of Duʾūzu, traces a ritual circuit
through Babylon — from the House of Lamentation, out along the road to Kūta, and
back through the Uraš Gate and the streets of the city to the Eturkalamma and the
crossing of the river. Each stop pairs a place with the recitations, actions, and
participants recorded in the cuneiform sources. Reconstruction after Pedersén, O.
(2021), *Babylon Maps*, Babylon: The Great City (Münster: Zaphon).
