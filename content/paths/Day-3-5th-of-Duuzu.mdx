The third day of the DLL ritual moves through southern inner Babylon, from the House of Lamentation to the Equlû, Ekagula, and Eḫursagtila. Recitations, songs, and laments accompany the procession, while the kurgarrû goes out through the Uraš Gate to sing toward Kiš and Ḫursagkalamma. In the streets, an overtly sexual performance honors Babylon’s two chief goddesses, while the assinnu honors Ninlil before the Esabad. Water libations likely appease Zarpānītu’s anger, after which the kurgarrû blesses King Marduk. The day ends with a procession to the river crossing near the Eturkalamma.
